Knee support strength coach

This routine is for people who want to strengthen the thigh and hip muscles that support the knees during standing, walking and using stairs. Follow the exercises recommended by your physiotherapist.

Safety first

These exercises are for general information only and are not medical advice. Check with your doctor or physiotherapist before starting if you have an injury, medical condition, recent surgery, significant knee pain, balance problems or mobility concerns. Stop if you develop pain, dizziness, unusual shortness of breath or feel unsteady.

They are not a treatment or cure for any knee condition.
